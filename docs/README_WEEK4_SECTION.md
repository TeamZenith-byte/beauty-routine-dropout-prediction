Week 4: Exploratory Data Analysis and Feature Engineering Week 4 is a planning and design report with clearly labelled SIMULATED / HYPOTHETICAL worked examples. It describes how the cleaned public product, ingredient and review data will be explored and which 23 features will be built. It does not contain real-data results: the Week 3 pipeline was tested only on simulated rows, and the real Sephora and Open Beauty Facts files have not been processed for this report. Public data have no verified routine_dropout label (that label is planned from the future survey and routine diary), BRFS is my own proposed framework without validated weights, and the five Week 1 hypotheses (H1 to H5) are still untested. The revised report (20 November 2026) adds worked examples on invented data: an EDA walkthrough, Spearman, Mann-Whitney U, Kruskal-Wallis, chi-square and Fisher calculations, ten chart specifications with four simulated figures, feature-engineering examples and further business scenarios. Every number in those examples comes from a script-generated SIMULATED table or from HYPOTHETICAL inputs. They are illustrations, not findings.
